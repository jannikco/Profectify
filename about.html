OUR APPROACH

Curiosity starts it.
Building makes it real.

Profectify is an independent, founder-led venture studio. We turn practical problems into products, then stay involved in the everyday work of running them.

A STUDIO THAT STAYS

From the first question
to the everyday details.

Our roots are in advisory: helping organisations work through people, operations and technology challenges. Building our own products brought those disciplines into one place.

Today, our portfolio spans creative tools, connected AI, learning platforms and business software. Each venture has its own purpose. What connects them is practical curiosity and a commitment to keep making them more useful.

01 / FIND THE FRICTION

Start with a real problem.

The best starting point is something that should be easier. We look for useful problems, close to the people doing the work.

02 / MAKE IT USEFUL

Build it. Use it. Improve it.

We bring product thinking, design and technology together. A useful first version gives us something real to learn from.

03 / STAY INVOLVED

Running it is part of building it.

Launch is a beginning. Support, feedback and everyday operations shape what comes next.

INDEPENDENT, TOGETHER

Designing a print editor teaches us about creative workflows. Connecting software teaches us about permissions and useful automation. Building learning products keeps us focused on clarity. That experience travels across the group.
